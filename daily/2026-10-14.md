#### <span data-idx="3"></span>Training Advisors for LLM Agents from Task Outcomes

> **从任务结果出发训练大模型智能体的顾问**

- **arXiv**：[2610.09858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09858) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09858) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09858v1)

- **作者备注**：26 pages, 11 figures
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：大语言模型智能体通过把推理与工具调用同来自环境的观测交织起来，来解决多步任务。已有工作表明，自然语言反馈能帮助这些智能体在执行任务过程中修正决策。我们提出 Caddie，一种训练评论者（critic）在智能体推进任务时提供自然语言分析与建议的方法。与依赖步骤级标签或参考批评的做法不同，Caddie 从「智能体在收到评论者反馈后最终是否成功」中学习。我们用强化学习优化评论者，同时保持基座模型冻结。以多跳问答为训练任务、使用单一基座模型，我们的 Qwen3-4B 评论者提升了四个不同规模与架构的基座模型的成功率，其中包括三个未参与评论者训练的模型。在 MuSiQue 基准上，训练后的评论者把 Qwen3-4B 的成功率提升超过 25 个百分点，超过了无评论者时 Kimi K3 的表现。同一评论者还在域外交互式基准（包括 ___ 与 DeepDive）上带来增益，且无需额外训练。这些结果表明，智能体可以在推理时自行决定何时向评论者求助，而基于结果的评论者训练能产生可跨基座模型与任务领域迁移的指导。

<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Large language model agents tackle multi-step tasks by interleaving reasoning and tool calls with observations from the environment. Prior work has shown that natural-language feedback can help these agents revise their decisions during task execution. We introduce Caddie, a method for training critics to provide natural-language analysis and advice as agents work through a task. Unlike approaches that rely on step-level labels or reference critiques, Caddie learns from whether the agent ultimately succeeds after receiving the critic's feedback. We optimize the critic through reinforcement learning while keeping the base model frozen. Trained on multi-hop question answering with a single base model, our Qwen3-4B critic improves success rates across four base models of different scales and architectures, including three not used during critic training. On the MuSiQue benchmark, the trained critic improves Qwen3-4B's success rate by more than 25 percentage points, surpassing the performance of Kimi K3 without a critic. The same critic also yields gains on out-of-domain interactive benchmarks, including $\tau^3$ and DeepDive, with no additional training. Our results show that agents can decide when to seek help from a critic at inference time and that outcome-based critic training can produce guidance that transfers across base models and task domains.

- **arXiv 分类**：Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI) ; Computation and Language (cs.CL); Machine Learning (cs.LG)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：智能体式大语言模型如今能处理复杂任务，但长时程上的策略推理仍研究不足：多数智能体基准短小且是回合式的，而长任务上的表现很难拆解为已有知识、推理与运气。我们提出 SpireBench：模型在其中玩完整的商业卡牌构筑游戏《杀戮尖塔》（StS），其中早期决策决定长期成败。在游戏最高难度下、横跨 20 个大语言模型（LLM）的测试中，前沿模型击败了启发式基线、并达到人类玩家平均水平，但仍远落后于高手。这一差距随回合延长而拉大：最强模型在第一幕的获胜频率与高手相当，随后便落后。强模型与弱模型的区别在于它们每次战斗打得多好；所有模型在整个流程中都会误判风险，倾向于保留生命值而不是消耗它来变强。对所有游戏术语做一次全面且自洽的重命名表明，它们的部分表现依赖于对该游戏的先验知识；而模型并不会从跨局写下的记忆中改进——这些记忆常常是错误且过度保守的。我们的 harness 是首个把完整原版游戏交给模型的，并与现有 harness 做了比较。总体而言，SpireBench 提供了一个尚未饱和的长时程基准，并配有用于定位「智能体在何处、为何失败」的工具。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Agentic large language models now tackle complex tasks, yet strategic reasoning over long horizons is under-studied: most agentic benchmarks are short and episodic, and performance on long tasks is hard to dissect into stored knowledge, reasoning and luck. We introduce SpireBench, in which models play the complete commercial deck-builder Slay the Spire (StS), where early decisions decide long-term success. Across 20 large language models (LLMs) at the game's highest difficulty, frontier models beat heuristic baselines and match the average human player but remain far behind experts. This gap widens as the run lengthens: the strongest models win the first act as often as experts, then fall behind. What separates strong models from weak is how well they play each fight; all of them misjudge risk over a run, preserving health rather than spending it to grow stronger. A comprehensive, self-consistent renaming of every game term shows that part of their performance rests on prior knowledge of the game, and models do not improve from the memories they write across runs, which are often wrong and overly cautious. Our harness is the first to give a model the complete original game, and we compare it with existing harnesses. Overall, SpireBench offers an unsaturated long-horizon benchmark with tools to locate where and why agents fail.

</details>


#### <span data-idx="5"></span>Goldsmith: Gold-Loss-Guided Definition Optimization with an Agentic Annotation Harness

> **Goldsmith：以黄金损失为引导的定义优化与智能体式标注 harness**

- **arXiv**：[2610.09489](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09489) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09489) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09489v1)
- 机构（T1）：Sun Yat-sen University ｜ 主要作者：Yihan Li（共 4 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Computation and Language (cs.CL)
- **作者备注**：20 pages, 4 figures, 11 tables. Accepted to the main conference of EMNLP 2026
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：许多标注项目在专家尚未形成稳定指南、也没有足够标签来训练任务专用模型时就已经开始。我们提出 Goldsmith，一条智能体式流水线，它把一个小规模黄金集——代表预期任务边界的专家标注校准样例——转化为可复用的结构化标注定义。Goldsmith 把该定义视为一个可训练的文本对象。候选定义在同一批黄金样例上运行，并用可执行的结构化损失打分，而输出模式、格式化、检索、修复、评判与人工复核都保留在外部 harness 中。一个大语言模型（LLM）编辑器把损失最高的失败转化为「文本梯度」式修订，且只有在实测损失下降时才被接受。在提示词优化对比中，Goldsmith 在匹配的评估协议下优于直接改写、OPRO、APE 与 PromptBreeder。所得定义在结合检索、基于分数的路由与人工复核后，也能提升下游标注效果，覆盖类型化跨度、成对关系与固定触发词事件论元等任务。这些结果表明，稀缺的专家监督既能支撑任务定义的学习，也能支撑可规模化的标注。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Many annotation projects begin before experts have a stable guideline or enough labels to train a task-specific model. We present Goldsmith, an agentic pipeline that turns a small gold set---expert-annotated calibration examples representing the intended task boundaries---into a reusable structured annotation definition. Goldsmith treats this definition as a trainable textual object. Candidate definitions are run on the same gold examples and scored with an executable structured loss, while the output schema, formatting, retrieval, repair, judging, and human review remain in an external harness. A large language model (LLM) editor converts the highest-loss failures into textual-gradient revisions, which are accepted only when the measured loss decreases. In prompt-optimization comparisons, Goldsmith improves over direct rewriting, OPRO, APE, and PromptBreeder under matched evaluation protocols. The resulting definition also improves downstream annotation when combined with retrieval, score-based routing, and human review across typed span, pair-level relation, and fixed-trigger event-argument tasks. <summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Test-time scaling (TTS) improves the reasoning capabilities of large language models by allocating additional inference computation. Existing approaches to improving TTS efficiency largely optimize accuracy against one resource dimension at a time, advancing either the accuracy--cost or accuracy--latency Pareto frontier. Yet user requirements are multidimensional: users may specify accuracy, latency, and inference-cost requirements jointly, and different requirements can favor different controllers. We formulate Personalized Test-Time Scaling as discovering executable controllers that maximize the joint satisfaction rate of user-specific requirements.
