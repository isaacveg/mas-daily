#### 3. MASTraceBench: Diagnosing Collaboration Gains through Proposal Trajectories in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems

- **摘要（中文）**：基于LLM的多智能体系统（MAS）在复杂问题解决中显示出前景。随着MAS方法的多样化，系统评估变得越来越具有挑战性。然而，现有的基准主要侧重于最终结果，不清楚协作成果是如何产生、保持或丧失的。为了解决这一限制，我们引入了MASTraceBench，这是通过MAS中的提案轨迹来诊断协作收益的基准。在六项合作和竞争性任务中，MASTraceBench跟踪和评分提案轨迹，并提供涵盖任务分数、协作增益、提案轨迹指标和令牌成本的多层指标套件。使用MASTraceBench，我们系统地比较了具有代表性的MAS方法，不仅包括最终性能，还包括客服代表建议如何演变并汇总成最终答案。这种分析揭示了一种反复出现的模式：最终的MAS答案很少超过最强的初始提案；互动往往会提升最初较弱的提案，而强大的初始提案很少得到进一步改善，并且可能会退步。为了降低这种风险，我们提出了CLEARS，它将整个提案交换替换为跨代理的索赔级评估，以指导可靠的合成。更频繁地清除保留或改进最强的初始提案，并在六项任务中的五项上实现最高的协作收益。
- **摘要（英文原文）**：<details><summary>展开原文</summary>LLM-based multi-agent systems (MAS) have shown promise in complex problem solving. As MAS methods diversify, systematic evaluation becomes increasingly challenging. However, existing benchmarks largely focus on final outcomes, leaving unclear how collaboration gains arise, are preserved, or are lost. To address this limitation, we introduce MASTraceBench, a benchmark for diagnosing collaboration gains through proposal trajectories in MAS. Across six cooperative and competitive tasks, MASTraceBench tracks and grades proposal trajectories and provides a multi-layer metric suite covering Task Score, Collaboration Gain, proposal-trajectory indicators, and Token Cost. Using MASTraceBench, we systematically compare representative MAS methods not only by final performance, but also by how agent proposals evolve and are aggregated into the final answer. This analysis reveals a recurring pattern: final MAS answers rarely surpass the strongest initial proposal; interaction often lifts initially weaker proposals toward it, while strong initial proposals are seldom further improved and may regress. To reduce this risk, we propose CLEARS, which replaces whole-proposal exchange with claim-level evaluation across agents to guide reliable synthesis. CLEARS more often preserves or improves upon the strongest initial proposal and achieves the highest Collaboration Gain on five of the six tasks.</details>

- **arXiv**：[2609.26781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26781) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.26781) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26781v1)

- **机构（T0）**：Microsoft Research
  - 抽取依据：`Microsoft Research`（来源：作者块）
- **arXiv 分类**：Computation and Language (cs.CL) ; Multiagent Systems (cs.MA)
- **作者备注**：13 pages, 6 figures
- **摘要（中文）**：多代理系统可以通过同时执行工作来减少复杂任务的延迟。几个开创性的线束框架支持多智能体系统。然而，当前多智能体线束的可扩展性通常受到中央编排人员分配任务和协调工作人员能力的限制。为了解决这一限制，我们推出了Agensh，这是一种可扩展的自组织多智能体线束，没有中央编排器：并发工作者执行多代理合作循环，不断收集上下文，声明并自行分配子任务，采取行动并共享发现，验证结果，并以异步方式合并进度。该循环由代理组织基础设施支持，包括三个组成部分：共享工作空间保存拟议、正在进行和已完成的工作；消息界面允许员工进行通信；和共享上下文保留了可重用的发现和工作意图。为了测试Agensh的可扩展性，我们使用GPT-5.6-sol（高）对五个最难的ProgramBench任务进行评估。从1种药物扩展到128种药物可将平均最终测试通过率从19.31%提高到28.78%，相对提高约49%。规模较大的组织可以更早地达到可比的测试通过率。在pandoc上，从1剂缩放到1,024剂将最终测试通过率从33.89%提高到55.06%。员工轨迹进一步表明，随着组织的发展，不同形式的自组织合作逐渐出现并标准化。这些结果揭示了代理数量作为多代理组织扩展通用智能前沿的新扩展维度，为硬延迟约束或时间预算下的复杂任务提供了实用解决方案。
- **摘要（英文原文）**：<details><summary>展开原文</summary>A multi-agent system can reduce latency on complex tasks by executing work concurrently. Several pioneering harness frameworks support multi-agent systems. However, the scalability of current multi-agent harnesses is often constrained by a central orchestrator's capacity to allocate tasks and coordinate workers. To address this limitation, we introduce Agensh, a scalable self-organized multi-agent harness without a central orchestrator: concurrent workers execute a multi-agent cooperation loop, continuously gathering context, claiming and self-assigning sub-tasks, taking action and sharing findings, verifying results, and merging progress in an asynchronous manner. The loop is supported by the agentic organization infrastructure comprising three components: a shared workspace holds proposed, ongoing, and completed work; a message interface lets workers communicate; and shared context retains reusable findings and work intentions.
