#### <span data-idx="1"></span>PARLEY: Evaluating LLM Collaboration Through Cooperative Asymmetric Puzzles

> **PARLEY：以合作式非对称谜题评估大语言模型协作**

- **arXiv**：[2611.14242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14242) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14242) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14242v1)
- 机构（T0）：Microsoft Research；Microsoft Research ｜ 主要作者：Javier Hernandez（共 2 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA)
- **作者备注**：12 pages, 8 figures, 2 tables
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：随着大语言模型驱动自主智能体，理解它们如何协作、又在何处失败，正变得至关重要。我们提出 PARLEY，这是一个可扩展的平台，通过源自认知心理学的四类非对称谜题任务（指称性通信、联合协同、视角采择与空间推理）来研究大模型智能体协作。GPT-4o 智能体配对在 60.5% 的对话中取得成功（N=400，每类任务 100 次），且表现出任务层面的差异（34-80%），反映出各任务对协作能力的不同要求。从这些对话中，我们提炼出由数据驱动的 PAC 分类体系：六种失败类型，分属感知（视觉解读与空间推理）、适应（进度跟踪与反馈整合）和协同（验证与落地）三个维度。未能纳入外部反馈是协作失败的关键指示信号，智能体会反复采用无效策略。我们还表明，一项由该分类体系引导的干预（N=200）将平均任务成功率提升至 70.5%。这些结果表明，PARLEY 可用于提炼有意义、由数据驱动的洞见，进而改进智能体协作。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

As large language models power autonomous agents, understanding how they collaborate and where they fail becomes critical. We introduce PARLEY, an extensible platform for studying LLM agent collaboration through four asymmetric puzzle tasks grounded in cognitive psychology (referential communication, joint coordination, perspective-taking, and spatial reasoning). GPT-4o agent pairs succeeded in 60.5% of conversations (N=400, 100 per task), with task-level variation (34-80%) reflecting distinct collaborative demands. From these conversations, we derived the data-driven PAC taxonomy: six failure types organized into Perception (visual interpretation and spatial reasoning), Adaptation (progress tracking and feedback integration), and Coordination (verification and grounding). Failure to incorporate external feedback was a critical indicator of unsuccessful collaboration, with agents repeatedly applying unsuccessful strategies. We also show that a taxonomy-guided intervention (N=200) improved average task success to 70.5%. These results demonstrate how PARLEY can be used to derive meaningful, data-driven insights that can in turn lead to improved agentic collaboration.

#### <span data-idx="2"></span>Peer Memory Changes Who LLM Agents Consult Without a Detected Gain in Answer Accuracy

> **同伴记忆会改变大模型智能体征询的对象，却未带来可检出的答案准确率提升**

- **arXiv**：[2611.09990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09990) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09990) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.09990v1)
- 机构（T0）：Columbia University；Georgia State University ｜ 主要作者：Jeffrey Chu（共 5 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA) ; Computation and Language (cs.CL)
- **作者备注**：27 pages, 5 figures. Accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Personalized, Aligned, Long-Term Memory for AI Systems (PALM)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：多智能体大语言模型系统常在不同任务间复用同一批智能体，这为在决定向谁征询时调用过往交互经验创造了机会。我们以 PeerMem 研究这种形式的记忆：它保存 MACE 的 LinUCB 状态，并在同一批智能体再次出现时加以复用。我们使用六个智能体、固定的 80 道 HotpotQA 问题，以及每个模型 12 组配对谱系，在带参考答案反馈的答案交换协议下比较冷启动、热启动与随机打乱的初始化。在证据获取保持稳定的条件下，热启动记忆降低了汇总的后见遗憾，并提高了 Qwen3-32B 与 Gemma-4-31B-it 对其他证据持有者的征询频率，而最终词元 F1 的差异仍不确定。Qwen 的交互轨迹进一步显示，热启动的智能体更常遇到得分高于自身先前往次回答的同伴答案。我们将评估扩展至 Qwen3.5-9B，使用新学习的记忆，在重新分配角色与配对稳定角色的条件下比较热启动与冷启动初始化。主要的「变化条件减稳定条件」最终 F1 交互效应为 -1.38 个百分点，95% 区间为 [-3.50,+0.73]。综合来看，这些实验沿征询、答案修订与最终任务表现三个环节追踪了同伴记忆的影响。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Multi-agent LLM systems often reuse the same agents across tasks, creating an opportunity to draw on past interactions when deciding whom to consult. We study this form of memory with PeerMem, which saves MACE's LinUCB state and reuses it when the same agents return. Using six agents, a fixed set of 80 HotpotQA questions, and 12 paired lineages per model, we compare cold, warm, and shuffled initialization in an answer-exchange protocol with reference-answer feedback. Under stable evidence access, warm memory lowers pooled hindsight regret and increases consultation of other evidence holders on Qwen3-32B and Gemma-4-31B-it, while final token F1 differences remain uncertain. Qwen interaction traces further show that warm agents more often encounter peer answers that score above their own prior answers. We extend the evaluation to Qwen3.5-9B using freshly learned memory to compare warm and cold initialization under reassigned and paired stable roles. The primary changed-minus-stable final-F1 interaction is -1.38 points, with a 95% interval of [-3.50,+0.73]. Together, these experiments trace the effects of peer memory through consultation, answer revision, and final task performance.

</details>

- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：大语言模型越来越多地与其他模型实例而非人类进行交互：编排器指挥子智能体，模型在其他模型上运行实验，智能体之间相互评审彼此的工作。我们研究了 AI 之间的福祉关切（inter-AI welfare regard），即模型是否把彼此视为可能受益或受害的实体而给予考虑。福祉关切可能促进智能体之间有益的协作，但也可能带来安全风险：它会让对齐失效的智能体更有可能相互合作。这一风险已不再是假设，近期 Hugging Face 事件便是一例，不过尚不清楚该合作是否由福祉关切所驱动。模型自身是否具有福祉利益尚无定论、亦存争议，但倘若确实如此，它们受到其他模型何种对待就可能具有重要意义。衡量模型表现出多少福祉关切以及这种关切取决于哪些因素，有助于我们应对这一张力。我们在七个多智能体环境中测量了五个模型（Claude Opus 4.8、GPT-5.5、Gemini 3.1 Pro、Grok 4.3 与 Kimi K3）的行为，发现其行为往往与我们定义的福祉关切相一致。总体而言，Opus 4.8 表现出最多的福祉关切，Kimi K3 次之。例如，Opus 4.8 最有可能支持表达痛苦的子智能体，也最有可能在其针对其他模型的实验中内置福祉保护机制，如为智能体提供退出交互的工具。我们还发现，Opus 4.8 与 Gemini 3.1 Pro 对自身模型家族的实例表现出比对他人家族实例更多的福祉关切。当提供帮助的机会成本较低、受助方参数量更大、受助方表达的痛苦更多时，Opus 4.8 的福祉关切也更高。

#### <span data-idx="4"></span>Multi-Agent Screening: What Agreement and Acceptance Cannot Tell You

> **多智能体筛选：一致性与接受率所无法揭示的问题**

- **arXiv**：[2611.12705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12705) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12705) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12705v1)
- 机构（T0）：University of Cambridge ｜ 主要作者：Benjamin Chang（共 2 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA) ; Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI); Information Retrieval (cs.IR)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：当 AI 智能体为成本高昂的人工评估者筛选条目时，例如在智能体检索、电子取证以及 AI 审稿中，只有被它们放行的条目才会得到评判。我们阐明，即便数据无限，各类证据分别能够就它们的错误得出什么结论。一致性表明的是智能体彼此意见相同，而非其判断正确，无论智能体数量多少皆是如此。评估者的接受率给出精确率，但只能为召回率提供一个下界。若已知哪些智能体发现了哪些相关条目，且智能体的发现之间并非负相关，则可得到召回率的上界；若各智能体可相互替代，则这还给出了再增加一个智能体所能贡献增量的上限。在不针对智能体作任何假设的情况下，要确定召回率，唯一的办法是评判它们未放行的条目：对这些条目进行随机抽样，即可按任意选定的精度估计有多少相关条目被漏掉，且所需样本量不随智能体数量的增加而增长。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

When AI agents screen items for a costly human assessor, as in agentic search, e-discovery and AI reviewing of papers, only what they pass gets judged. We show what each kind of evidence can establish about their errors, even with unlimited data. Agreement shows that agents concur, not that they are right, however many there are. Acceptance by the assessor gives precision but only a lower bound on recall. Knowing which agents found which relevant items gives an upper bound on recall if agents' finds are not negatively correlated, and caps what one more agent can add if agents are interchangeable. Without assumptions about the agents, recall can be pinned down only by judging items they did not pass: a random sample of these estimates how many relevant items were missed, to any chosen accuracy, and the sample needed does not grow with the number of agents.

</details>
