#### <span data-idx="1"></span>Formalizing the Moral Evaluation of Speech Acts: Truthfulness, Lies and Ethical Dilemmas

- **arXiv**：[2610.04747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04747) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.04747) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04747v1)

- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：在生死攸关的情境中，善意的谎言可能比说真话显得更道德。然而这类谎言可能适得其反，造成意料之外、有时甚至是致命的后果。这一张力曾由康德与贡斯当在 1797 年著名地争论过；它不只适用于说谎，也适用于一般性的断言性言语行为，由此引出的问题是：当道德代价很高时应当选择哪一种表述。我们提出一个基于智能体信念、用于言语行为表述之伦理评价的逻辑框架。该框架用回答集编程（ASP）实现，可在义务论、后果论与原则主义下评估表述，并以萨特《墙》（1939）为例加以说明——该作正是上述争论的改写，其中说谎交替导致获救与死亡。我们的设定在设计上具有一般性：如两个变体所示，容纳新的道德情境只需调整参数，而无需改动规则。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

In life-or-death situations, a benevolent lie may appear more moral than telling the truth. Yet such lies can backfire, producing unintended and sometimes fatal consequences. This tension, famously disputed by Kant and Constant in 1797, applies not only to lying but to assertive speech acts in general, raising the question of which utterance should be chosen when moral stakes are high. We present a logical framework for the ethical evaluation of speech-act utterances based on agents' beliefs. Implemented in Answer Set Programming (ASP), the framework assesses utterances under deontologism, consequentialism, and principialism, and is illustrated on Sartre's The Wall (1939), a reworking of that controversy in which lying leads alternately to rescue and to death. Our setting is general by design: as two variants show, accommodating a new moral situation amounts to adjusting parameters, not rules.

#### <span data-idx="4"></span>Communication Shapes Collective Inference in Self-Adapting LLM Societies: Evidence from Mafia

> **通信塑造自适应大语言模型社会中的集体推断：来自 Mafia 的证据**

- **arXiv**：[2610.05041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05041) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.05041) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05041v1)
- 机构（T0）：Princeton University；New York University ｜ 主要作者：Haonan Huang（共 2 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA) ; Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI); Computation and Language (cs.CL)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：通信何时有助于一个群体识别隐藏的对手，其价值又如何随群体自适应而变化？在 Mafia 中，掌握信息的少数派隐藏在不掌握信息的多数派之中，后者唯一的证据只来自公开博弈。零信息博弈中每日投票随机淘汰一名玩家，该博弈已被精确求解，并可为每个社会打分；协议之间在演员阵容匹配条件下的比较则用于识别通信的作用。由 8 至 100 个 claude-haiku-4-5 智能体构成的社会通过改写与继承私人策略笔记实现自适应（分析 7,416 局游戏，共 1.9M 次模型调用）。在所测试的全部九种构成（8 至 46 名玩家）中，同时广播相较沉默均提升了对对手的识别。轮流发言则消除了这一优势的大部分；其投票压倒性一致出现的频率与广播相当，但命中黑手党的概率接近随机（1.08 倍对 2.53 倍）。在 70 名玩家时，每天阅读八条陈述的智能体对对手的识别反而不如沉默的智能体，有限发言的价值也低于 46 名玩家时。自适应虽快，却未必有益。在首次广播对局中，平民宣告自身身份的频率远高于黑手党（91% 对 30%），首日投票命中黑手党的概率为随机水平的三倍；而在两代之内平民便不再宣告身份，这一线索随之消退，而该变化由继承下来的笔记所承载。在 16 名玩家的受控重新部署中，携带自身六十代笔记的社会得分低于完全不携带笔记的社会。通信既塑造集体推断，也塑造其所依赖的信号，因此协议的价值必须与改变这些信号的自适应过程一并衡量。

<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

When does communication help a group identify hidden adversaries, and how does its value change as the group adapts? In Mafia, an informed minority hides inside an uninformed majority whose only evidence is open play. The zero-information game, where each day's vote eliminates a random player, is exactly solved and scores every society; matched-casting comparisons between protocols identify the effect of communication. Societies of 8-100 claude-haiku-4-5 agents (7,416 analyzed games, 1.9M model calls) adapt by rewriting and inheriting private strategy notes. The CADENCE algorithm solves the Partially Observable Cooperative Guard Art Gallery Problem (POCGAGP) with formal coverage and connectivity guarantees, but leaves unspecified which valid corner each agent should be deployed to, a choice that strongly affects efficiency. We introduce two learned corner-selection heuristics that preserve these guarantees: a CNN scoring candidates on a grid encoding, and a GATv2 network trained with Deep Q-Learning (DQN) on a visibility graph.
