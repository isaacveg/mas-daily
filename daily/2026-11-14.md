#### <span data-idx="2"></span>MULTILOOP: Evolving Multi-Agent Harnesses for Complex Information-Seeking Tasks

> **MULTILOOP：为复杂信息检索任务演化多智能体 harness**

- **arXiv**：[2611.15754](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15754) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15754) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.15754v1)
- 机构（T0）：Google Research ｜ 主要作者：Daniela Gottesman（共 7 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA)
- **作者备注**：Accepted to NeurIPS 2026 CL4FMAgents Workshop
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：基于大语言模型的智能体的性能不仅取决于底层模型，还取决于 harness：即围绕模型构建的提示词、工具与编排逻辑。近期工作借助编码智能体实现 harness 优化的自动化，由后者分析执行轨迹、诊断失败并修改 harness 代码；但这些工作聚焦于单智能体系统。多智能体 harness 更难以优化，因为修复它们除了要改变每个智能体的行为方式，还需要改变智能体之间的交互方式。我们以 DeepSearchQA [Gupta et al., 2026] 中的复杂信息检索任务为案例，研究自动化的 harness 优化能否诊断并修复多智能体系统中的失败。我们应用 MULTILOOP，这是一个简单的优化循环，其中的编码智能体会迭代地分析、评估并修改 harness。仅使用 10 个训练样本，演化后的 harness 相比基础 harness 将准确率提升了 4.6%，相比最佳单智能体系统提升了 3.2%。然而，对优化前后失败模式的分类分析显示，体现多智能体系统特征的编排类失败在很大程度上依然存在。我们的发现为激励智能体委派任务与共享信息的结构性改变的 harness 优化方法提供了动机。

<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

The performance of LLM-based agents depends not only on the underlying model but also on the harness: the prompts, tools, and orchestration logic around it. Recent works automate harness optimization with coding agents that analyze execution traces, diagnose failures, and edit the harness code; however, those focused on single-agent systems. Multi-agent harnesses are harder to optimize as fixing them requires changing how agents interact in addition to how each behaves. We study whether automated harness optimization can diagnose and repair failures in multi-agent systems through a case study on complex information-seeking tasks from DeepSearchQA [Gupta et al., 2026]. We apply MULTILOOP, a simple optimization loop in which a coding agent iteratively analyzes, evaluates and edits the harness. With only 10 training samples, the evolved harness improves accuracy by 4.6% over the base harness and by 3.2% over the best single-agent system. However, a taxonomy of failure modes before and after optimization shows that the orchestration failures that characterize multi-agent systems largely persist. Our findings motivate harness optimization methods that encourage structural changes to how agents delegate tasks and share information.

#### <span data-idx="3"></span>From Delegation to Coordination: Learning Efficient Multi-Agent Deep Research

> **从委派到协同：学习高效的多智能体深度研究**

- **arXiv**：[2611.17041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17041) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17041) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17041v1)
- 机构（T0）：Zhejiang University；Ant Group；University of Electronic Science and Technology of China ｜ 主要作者：Yuyang Wu（共 11 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA) ; Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI)
- **作者备注**：16 pages, 3 figures, 3 tables
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：多智能体深度研究系统通过委派来并行开展检索，并使冗长的工具调用轨迹不进入主智能体的上下文。然而，只有当任务分配与子任务执行得到协同时，委派才会带来收益：主智能体必须发出有用的子任务，而子智能体必须返回可靠的证据。现有训练要么只优化主智能体，要么把同一团队优势广播给所有智能体，这可能奖励误导性的子智能体报告、却惩罚有用的报告。我们提出 LEAD-RL（Learning Effective Agent Delegation with Reinforcement Learning），这是一个带有贡献感知信用分配的、主智能体与子智能体联合训练的框架。训练阶段的评分量表会评估每个子智能体报告的研究效用，并据此对其继承的团队优势进行门控，而最终答案的正确性始终是唯一的团队奖励；不使用任何词元、步数或加速比奖励。在五个基准和两个模型家族上，LEAD-RL 提升了答案质量。在 BrowseComp 上，与共享团队信用的联合训练相比，它将准确率提升了 2.49 个百分点，并将关键路径开销降低了 24.5%。在验证阶段，准确率提升了 6.78 个点，而关键路径开销基本保持不变。这些结果表明，贡献感知的信用分配改善了多智能体深度研究的质量—效率权衡。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Multi-agent deep-research systems use delegation to parallelize search and keep long tool traces out of the main agent's context. Yet delegation helps only when task allocation and subtask execution are coordinated: the main agent must issue useful subtasks, while subagents must return reliable evidence. Existing training either optimizes only the main agent or broadcasts the same team advantage to all agents, which can reward misleading subagent reports and penalize useful ones. We introduce LEAD-RL (Learning Effective Agent Delegation with Reinforcement Learning), a joint main-agent-subagent training framework with contribution-aware credit assignment. A training-time rubric evaluates the research utility of each subagent report and gates its inherited team advantage, while final-answer correctness remains the only team reward; no token, step, or speedup reward is used. Across five benchmarks and two model families, LEAD-RL improves answer quality. On BrowseComp, it improves accuracy by 2.49 percentage points and reduces critical-path cost by 24.5% over joint training with shared team credit. During validation, accuracy increases by 6.78 points at approximately constant critical-path cost. These results show that contribution-aware credit assignment improves the quality-efficiency trade-off of multi-agent deep research.

</details>

#### <span data-idx="5"></span>CapLite: Prompt-Injection Defense for Small Language Model Agents without Model-Written Security Artifacts

> **CapLite：无需模型自撰安全构件的面向小语言模型智能体的提示词注入防御**

- **arXiv**：[2611.17115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17115) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17115) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17115v1)
- 机构（T0）：University of Tokyo；Institute of Automation, CAS ｜ 主要作者：Zimo Xu（共 3 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI) ; Computation and Language (cs.CL); Cryptography and Security (cs.CR)
- **作者备注**：19 pages, 11 figures, 9 tables
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：针对提示词注入的执行类防御，例如 CaMeL、Progent、IPIGuard 与 DRIFT，都要求智能体自身的模型编写一份安全构件（程序、权限策略、依赖图或约束列表），再由代码来强制执行。我们追问：小语言模型（SLM）智能体是否可信地编写这些构件。在 15 个开放权重模型中（其中 13 个参数量介于 1.7B 与 12B 之间），答案是否定的：由智能体模型编写的策略、图和约束在 14 个（Progent）、13 个（IPIGuard）和 11 个（DRIFT）模型上均可被攻击绕过，而 CaMeL 的程序在多达 68% 的回合中无法运行。我们提出 CapLite，它从工具模式（tool schema）中推导策略，在读取任何工具结果之前即提交写入调用，并且只有当决定其影响对象或内容的每个参数都在用户请求中明确陈述时，才允许写入。这一做法保证写入完整性：注入的文本无法新增写入，任何写入也不会到达用户未曾陈述的接收者、金额、对象或目的地。在 13,935 个受攻击的 AgentDojo 回合中，没有任何攻击得手。CapLite 在遭受攻击时的可用性（utility）与 CaMeL 相当（29.0% 对 27.2%），且词元开销低于其他所有执行类防御，也低于无防御的智能体：每回合 17.1k，为 CaMeL 的 18%，并在所有执行类防御中拥有最高的每词元可用性。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Enforcement defenses against prompt injection, such as CaMeL, Progent, IPIGuard and DRIFT, ask the agent's own model to write a security artifact (a program, a privilege policy, a dependency graph or a constraint list) that code then enforces. We ask whether small language model (SLM) agents can be trusted to write these artifacts. Across 15 open-weight models, 13 of them between 1.7B and 12B parameters, they cannot: the policies, graphs and constraints that the agent's model writes admit attacks on 14 (Progent), 13 (IPIGuard) and 11 (DRIFT) of the models, and CaMeL's programs fail to run in up to 68% of episodes. We present CapLite, which derives its policy from the tool schemas, commits the write calls before any tool result is read, and admits a write only if every argument that decides whom or what it affects is stated in the user's request. This guarantees write integrity: injected text cannot add a write, and no write reaches a recipient, amount, object or destination that the user did not state. No attack succeeds in 13,935 attacked AgentDojo episodes. CapLite reaches utility under attack comparable to CaMeL's (29.0% vs. 27.2%) with fewer tokens than every other enforcement defense and than the undefended agent: 17.1k per episode, 18% of CaMeL's, and the highest utility per token of all enforcement defenses.

</details>
