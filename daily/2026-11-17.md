- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：多智能体系统通过把复杂任务分解为子任务、把子任务委派给各智能体、并用一条聚合规则汇总各智能体的响应来求解问题。恶意智能体可以通过对其被指派的子任务给出恶意响应来操纵最终解。本文首先提出响应操纵攻击（ReMA），它迭代优化恶意响应以最大化攻击效果。具体而言，我们把无目标攻击与有目标攻击表述为优化问题，并基于来自代理多智能体系统的反馈迭代精炼恶意响应，从而近似求解这些问题。为防御此类攻击，我们进一步提出 SecMAS——一条安全聚合规则，它利用各智能体响应之间的语义相似度，选出与「足够大的其他响应子集」最为一致的那条响应。我们从理论上为 SecMAS 建立了对无目标与有目标两类攻击的安全保证。大量评估表明，现有聚合规则对 ReMA 脆弱，而 SecMAS 能有效防御所有受评攻击（包括 ReMA 的自适应变体），同时保持与现有聚合规则相当的效用。

<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Multi-agent systems solve complex tasks by decomposing them into subtasks, delegating the subtasks to agents, and aggregating the agents' responses using an aggregation rule. Malicious agents can manipulate the final solution by providing malicious responses to their assigned subtasks. In this paper, we first propose response manipulation attacks (ReMA), which iteratively optimize malicious responses to maximize attack effectiveness. Specifically, we formulate untargeted and targeted attacks as optimization problems and develop an approach to approximately solve these problems by iteratively refining malicious responses based on feedback from a surrogate multi-agent system. To defend against such attacks, we further propose SecMAS, a secure aggregation rule that leverages the semantic similarities among agent responses to select the response that is most consistent with a sufficiently large subset of the other responses. We theoretically establish security guarantees for SecMAS against both untargeted and targeted attacks. Extensive evaluations demonstrate that existing aggregation rules are vulnerable to ReMA, whereas SecMAS effectively defends against all evaluated attacks, including adaptive variants of ReMA, while maintaining utility comparable to that of existing aggregation rules.

<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

We study collective tree exploration with subtree-size predictions. In this problem, a team of $k$ mobile agents needs to explore an unknown rooted tree as fast as possible, and whenever a new vertex $u$ is discovered, the team receives a prediction $\hat n_u$ of the number of nodes $n_u$ in the subtree rooted at $u$. We provide algorithmic guarantees under two error measures: the maximum error $E=\max_u|\widehat n_u-n_u|$ and the multiplicative distortion $\mu = (\max_u\widehat n_u/n_u)(\max_u n_u/\widehat n_u)$. For the maximum error, we present a deterministic algorithm that explores any tree with $n$ nodes and depth $D$ in $O(\text{OPT}+E)$ rounds, where $\text{OPT} = \Theta(n/k+D)$ is the offline benchmark, and we show that there is no deterministic algorithm that admits an $O(\text{OPT})+o(E)$ guarantee. For the multiplicative distortion, we present several algorithms that induce a competitive ratio in $\widetilde O(\min\{ \sqrt\mu\log k, \mu\log\log k+\mu^{1.5} \})$, where the $\widetilde O(\cdot)$ may hide logarithmic factors in $\mu$.
