- **主线一｜"全 AI 招聘"闭环里出现了可测量的不公平（本期最值得读）**：*AI evaluators favor job applications written by certain AI models* 用 **11 个撰写者智能体 × 9 个评估者智能体 × 10 份职位 × 每份 50 名候选人**的审计框架，发现一个**生成偏好层级**：按评估者接受率对撰写模型形成一致的全局排序，其中**本地部署的模型被系统性歧视（无论许可证如何）**，并叠加「评估者偏好自己的输出」的自偏好偏差。后果是可量化的：**在最不利的配对中，25 个合格候选人里约 1.8 个纯粹因模型不匹配被筛掉**。机制上，撰写者偏好与自偏好都可追溯到"各撰写者依赖特定文体线索的程度"与"评估者是否一致奖励这些线索"的组合。
- **主线二｜多工具检索的完备性远比想象中差**：*Completeness and Cost of Retrieval Structures for Multi-Tool Requests* 在 345 条 AppWorld 查询与 103 个 MCP-Bench 任务上比较六类检索结构，给出两组关键数字：**completeness@10 在单目标查询上是 0.58–0.67，而在含四个及以上目标的查询上只有 0.08–0.17**，且**检索器在单目标上的排序会在多目标上反转**；此外，所有"未在测试台上训练、每查询至多一次 LLM 调用"的结构，在 k=16 时完成的 AppWorld 查询**都不超过 10%**。下游检验更直白：**检索集完整时任务 21/21 成功，不完整时仅 2/39 成功**。
- **主线三｜记忆的两类失效需要相反的写入规则**：*Accept Changes, Reject Attacks* 指出记忆管理器会以两种方式出问题——保留已变更事实（过时）与存储被植入内容（投毒），而**这两者要求的写入决策恰恰相反**，所以没有固定的写入规则能同时处理。作者用「证据条件化准确率」（ECA）来度量，并诚实报告：**现有记忆系统与防御并不比未调优的管理器更鲁棒**，其一次性微调方案虽把总体 ECA 翻倍以上，但"鲁棒性仍不完整"。
- **主线四｜监管驱动的研究与异构算力/网络场景的工程收益**：*When Disclosure Is Compulsory* 针对欧盟《平台工作指令》（2026 年 12 月前转化为各国法律），用自利型 LLM 司机智能体比较反应式与前瞻式调度政策，发现**"完全遵从下表现相同的政策，在强制披露下可能给出相反结果"**（曼哈顿场景中两政策利润最终相差 6.1%）。工程侧，*RAMA*（LEO 卫星路由）在 1,584 颗卫星的 Starlink 第一壳层上把平均延迟降低 **93.2%**；*MetaVul* 显示**用一个模型优化出的 harness 迁移到另一模型即可把留出集成对准确率提升 14.7 个点**。

#### <span data-idx="4"></span>How a group that cannot communicate knows itself by its enemy

> **无法通信的团队如何借助敌手认识自己**

- **arXiv**：[2610.40835](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40835) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40835) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40835v1)
- 机构（T0）：Cornell University ｜ 主要作者：Gregory Falco
- **arXiv 分类**：Computer Science and Game Theory (cs.GT) ; Multiagent Systems (cs.MA); Robotics (cs.RO); Systems and Control (eess.SY)
- **作者备注**：6 pages main text; 35-page SI Appendix included as an ancillary file. Code and data: this https URL
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：无法通信的团队似乎只能受限于每个成员各自所见。我们表明，其对手能告诉他们其余部分。沉默的猎人、被干扰的机器人、从不相见的交易者，都面对一个将群体作为整体来反应的对手；每个成员都能读懂那个反应——一个「提示」——并学到任何成员独自都不知道的东西。一个提示可以完成「每个成员各发一条消息」的工作。对手更愿隐藏，但隐藏会干扰它自己的任务，而一个预先承诺其反应方式的团队决定了隐藏值多少。一个作出承诺的团队，在对手更在意自身目标时永不失败；并且当对手的利害超过撒谎所得时，它能做到与完全知情团队一样的表现。低于该点时，团队通过「更少信任」学到更多：在一个已求解的高斯模型中，它通过承诺「不会用上全部真相」来获得真相，并保住了对手本应为隐瞒付出的代价；一个精确检验决定了这在有限博弈中何时成立。团队如何分配信任同样重要。一个拥有全支撑先验的孤立无知情成员需要一枚私有硬币；但在加性平方误差损失下，两个反应不同的可互换成员就能替代它，而对信任打个小折扣可防止误判敌人。在这个视角下，信息不仅是被采集或聚合的，它是被团队「承诺如何反应」的方式生产出来的。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Teams that cannot communicate seem stuck with what each member sees. We show that their opponent can tell them the rest. Silent hunters, jammed robots and traders who never meet all face an adversary that reacts to the group as a whole; every member can read that reaction, a tell, and learn what no member knows alone. One tell can do the work of a message from every member. The adversary would rather hide, but hiding interferes with its own task, and a team that commits in advance to how it will respond decides how much hiding is worth. A committed team never loses when its opponent cares more about its own goals, and does as well as a fully informed team exactly when the opponent's stake outweighs any gain from lying. Below that point the team learns more by trusting less: in a solved Gaussian model it obtains the truth by promising not to use all of it and keeps the cost the opponent would have paid to conceal; an exact test decides when this holds in finite games. How a team spreads its trust matters too. A lone uninformed member with a full-support prior needs a private coin, but with additive squared-error losses two interchangeable members who respond differently replace it, and a small discount on trust insures against misjudging the enemy. Information, on this view, is not only gathered or aggregated; it is produced by how a team commits to respond.

</details>

#### <span data-idx="22"></span>RAMA: Retrieval-Augmented Multi-Agent Routing with Episodic Memory for LEO Satellite Networks

> **RAMA：面向低轨卫星网络的、带情景记忆的检索增强多智能体路由**

- **arXiv**：[2610.42068](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42068) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.42068) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.42068v1)
- 机构（T1）：University of Waterloo ｜ 主要作者：Xin Ming Li（共 2 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Networking and Internet Architecture (cs.NI) ; Machine Learning (cs.LG)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：低轨（LEO）卫星网络因快速变化的拓扑、流量与链路条件而构成具有挑战性的路由环境。现有深度强化学习（DRL）路由方法通常依赖当前观测与编码在网络权重中的信息，限制了其利用成功过往经验的能力。我们提出 RAMA，一个带近似信息状态（AIS）的检索增强多智能体 DRL 框架，其中每颗卫星把其局部观测、近期路由历史、邻居状态信息与从该卫星专属情景记忆中检索到的条目结合起来。检索到的经验通过一个以 AIS 辅助目标训练的 Transformer 决策网络加以整合。在一个 140 颗卫星的开普勒星座上冻结权重后，RAMA 相对当前最佳的双深度 Q 网络（DDQN）路由方法把平均端到端延迟降低 13.4%、把 99 分位延迟降低 66.7%，主要方式是避免长绕路。在线更新时，RAMA 保持稳定而 DDQN 退化。在含 1,584 颗卫星的 Starlink 第一壳层上，平均延迟的降幅增至 93.2%，而在线更新 RAMA 又使其平均延迟进一步降低 25.5%。这些结果证明了检索增强记忆在 LEO 卫星网络可扩展去中心化路由中的有效性。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks present a challenging routing environment due to rapidly changing topology, traffic, and link conditions. Existing deep reinforcement learning (DRL) routing methods typically rely on current observations and information encoded in network weights, limiting their ability to exploit successful past experiences. We propose RAMA, a retrieval-augmented multi-agent DRL framework with Approximate Information State (AIS), in which each satellite combines its local observation, recent routing history, neighboring state information, and retrieved entries from a per-satellite episodic memory. Retrieved experiences are integrated through a Transformer-based decision network trained with AIS-based auxiliary objectives. With weights frozen in a 140-satellite Kepler constellation, RAMA reduces mean end-to-end latency by 13.4% and 99th-percentile latency by 66.7% relative to the state-of-theart double deep Q-network (DDQN) routing method, mainly by avoiding long detours. Updated online, RAMA remains stable while DDQN degrades. On the 1,584-satellite Starlink shell 1, the reduction in mean latency grows to 93.2%, and updating RAMA online lowers its mean latency by a further 25.5%. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of retrieval-augmented memory for scalable decentralized routing in LEO satellite networks.
