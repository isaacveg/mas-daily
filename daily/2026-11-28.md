#### <span data-idx="2"></span>LSRP*-LA: Scalable and Anytime Multi-Agent Path Finding for Large Agents with Asynchronous Actions

> **LSRP*-LA：面向大型智能体与异步动作的可扩展任意时间多智能体路径规划**

- **arXiv**：[2611.33793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33793) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33793) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33793v1)
- 机构（T0）：Shanghai Jiao Tong University ｜ 主要作者：Dachuan Liu（共 3 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Robotics (cs.RO) ; Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI); Multiagent Systems (cs.MA)
- **作者备注**：8 pages, 10 figures. Submitted to IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：多智能体路径规划（MAPF）要为多个智能体寻找从起点到终点的无碰撞路径，并使路径代价最小。多数 MAPF 算法假定每个智能体只占据单个顶点、且所有智能体的动作是同步的；这些假设可能限制 MAPF 规划器在实际中的使用。现有规划器若想摆脱这两个假设，要么只能处理少数智能体，要么无法继续改进解。最近提出的 LSRP* 具备可扩展性与任意时间性（anytime），并支持异步动作，但仍受「每个智能体只占一个顶点」的限制。本文提出新的规划器 LSRP*-LA，面向**大型智能体 + 异步动作**的 MAPF（LA-MAPF-AA），其中单个智能体可占据多个格子。LSRP*-LA 既可扩展又是完备的，并且在得到初始解之后会持续改进解的质量，直到运行时间预算耗尽。我们分析了 LSRP*-LA 的性质，并在多种地图上与若干基线对比，智能体数量最多达 800。LSRP*-LA 可处理的智能体数量是基线方法的两倍，其解代价约为近期大型智能体同步规划器的一半。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Multi-Agent Path Finding (MAPF) seeks collision-free paths for multiple agents from their start locations to their goal locations while minimizing path costs. Most MAPF algorithms assume that each agent occupies a single vertex and that the actions of all agents are synchronized. These assumptions may limit the use of MAPF planners in practice. Existing planners that get rid of both assumptions either handle only a few agents or cannot improve their solutions. Recently, a planner called LSRP* was proposed, which is scalable and anytime with asynchronous actions, but still limited by that each agent occupies a single vertex. This paper develops a new planner called LSRP*-LA for MAPF for large agents with asynchronous actions (LA-MAPF-AA), where an agent can occupy several cells. LSRP*-LA is scalable and complete, and it keeps improving solution quality after the initial solution is obtained until the runtime budget depletes. We analyze the properties of LSRP*-LA and test it against several baselines with up to 800 agents on various maps. LSRP*-LA can handle twice as many agents as baseline methods and its solution cost is about half that of recent synchronous planners for large agents.

</details>


#### <span data-idx="3"></span>What Must LLM Agents Communicate? From Information Loss to Communication Failures

- **arXiv**：[2611.33318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33318) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33318) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33318v1)
- 机构（T0）：Carnegie Mellon University；McGill University ｜ 主要作者：Yi-Yun Chu（共 2 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Multiagent Systems (cs.MA) ; Information Theory (cs.IT); Machine Learning (cs.LG)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：当证据分散在多个 LLM 智能体之间时，一个团队可能事实上已掌握决策所需的全部信息，却仍无法有效利用它。要让联合预测准确，智能体**必须**传递什么？交互又在何时真正有帮助？我们研究有效通信的三项要求：所传递信息的**充分性**、相关证据的**选取**，以及所收信息的**利用**。借助信息论与通信复杂度，我们建立了一个把理论基准、受控实验与消息级分析结合起来的框架。我们发现，**本地概率报告会丢弃关键信息**，而理论上的信息损失与实际观察到的性能损失高度吻合。当相关证据依赖于某个私有查询时，**「先问后答」（query-first）的交互**能改善预测；但紧凑的一次性表示会抹掉这一优势。即便传递了充分的信息，智能体也可能无法正确选取或使用它。这些发现合在一起，把**根本性的通信上限**与**LLM 智能体在通信与信息利用方式上的失效**区分了开来。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

When evidence is distributed across LLM agents, a team may collectively possess all the information needed for a decision yet fail to use it effectively. What must agents communicate for accurate joint predictions, and when does interaction help? We study three requirements for effective communication: sufficiency of the information conveyed, selection of relevant evidence, and utilization of received information. Drawing on information theory and communication complexity, we develop a framework that combines theoretical benchmarks with controlled experiments and message-level analyses. We find that local probability reports can discard essential information, with theoretical information loss closely matching observed performance penalties. Query-first interaction improves predictions when relevant evidence depends on a private query, but compact one-shot representations can eliminate this advantage. Even when sufficient information is communicated, agents may fail to select or use it correctly. Together, these findings distinguish fundamental communication limits from failures in how LLM agents communicate and use information.

</details>

- **作者备注**：44 pages, 10 figures, 24 tables
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：强化学习在序贯决策上取得了显著成功，但要学到有效策略往往需要大量交互经验。基于模型的强化学习借助世界模型缓解了这一依赖，而世界模型通常被用来支持规划或策略优化。本文研究一种替代范式：由自主的、基于 LLM 的编码智能体，通过对**可读的世界模型**进行推理，直接合成控制策略。这些智能体阅读环境源码，把环境知识转译为可执行的决策规则，并用在线反馈或离线数据迭代精修其策略，而**无需针对特定任务的 RL 训练**。在多种决策基准上的实验表明，合成出的策略在经验需求显著更少的情况下，达到或超越了常规 RL 方法。进一步分析揭示了可解释的决策规则，以及它们如何经由反馈驱动的精修而演化。与「看不到源码」所生成的策略的对比、以及困难条件下的实验，进一步印证了可读世界模型的价值。这些发现表明，直接策略合成能够降低对试错的依赖，从而对「针对特定任务的 RL 训练是否必要」提出了挑战。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Reinforcement learning has achieved remarkable success in sequential decision-making, but learning effective policies often requires substantial interaction experience. Model-based reinforcement learning alleviates this dependence by leveraging world models, which are typically used to support planning or policy optimization. In this paper, we investigate an alternative paradigm in which autonomous LLM-based coding agents directly synthesize control policies by reasoning over readable world models. By examining environment source code, these agents translate environmental knowledge into executable decision rules and iteratively refine their policies using online feedback or offline data, without task-specific RL training. Experiments across diverse decision-making benchmarks show that synthesized policies match or outperform conventional RL methods while requiring substantially less experience. Further analyses reveal interpretable decision rules and how they evolve through feedback-driven refinement. Comparisons with policies generated without source code access and experiments under challenging conditions further demonstrate the benefits of readable world models. These findings suggest that direct policy synthesis can reduce reliance on trial and error, challenging the necessity of task-specific RL training.

#### <span data-idx="5"></span>Formalization, Transparency, and Collusion in Open-Source Game Theory

> **开源博弈论中的形式化、透明性与合谋**

- **arXiv**：[2611.33193](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33193) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33193) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33193v1)
- 机构（T0）：ETH Zurich；University of Oxford；Vector Institute；Max Planck Institute ｜ 主要作者：Colomban Duclaux（共 6 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Computer Science and Game Theory (cs.GT) ; Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI); Multiagent Systems (cs.MA); Theoretical Economics (econ.TH)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：当 AI 智能体之间的合作以**合谋**的形式出现时，就会破坏对齐协议。在这些协议下，智能体往往各自掌握关于对方的一些信息，而掌握多少可能决定它们是否合谋。开源博弈论研究其极端情形：每个程序都读取对手的源码，并据此对其所能证成的性质采取行动。我们为该理论奠定形式化基础：指定一个具体的证明系统，用换位论证解决了一个涉及 DupocBot 与 CupodBot 的开放问题，并在 Lean 4 中机械化了该框架——据我们所知，这是**资源受限开源博弈论的首次机械化**。我们随后把框架扩展到更现实的**部分透明**情形，即每个程序只获得关于对手的部分信息。在「相互合作即合谋」的囚徒困境实验中，更高的透明性同时提高了均衡中可持续的合作水平、以及复制子动态所选择出的合作水平。这表明，**控制智能体之间能知道多少，对对齐协议至关重要**。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Cooperation among AI agents will undermine alignment protocols when it takes the form of collusion. Under these protocols, agents often have some information about one another, and how much they have may determine whether they collude. Open-source game theory studies the extreme case, in which each program reads its opponent's source code and acts based on what it can prove about it. We develop formal foundations for this theory: we specify a concrete proof system, resolve an open problem concerning DupocBot and CupodBot through a transposition argument, and mechanize the framework in Lean 4; to our knowledge, this is the first mechanization of resource-bounded open-source game theory. We then extend the framework to the more realistic setting of partial transparency, where each program receives only partial information about its opponent. In Prisoner's Dilemma experiments, where mutual cooperation models collusion, greater transparency increases both the cooperation sustainable in equilibrium and the cooperation selected by replicator dynamics. This suggests that controlling how much agents can know about one another matters for alignment protocols.

</details>
