<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

We study theoretically how discrimination and polarization can emerge in a population of learning agents. Each agent uses an adaptive linear decision layer to map a language-model embedding of an issue to a binary opinion and maintains an evolving estimate of how much it trusts every other agent. Through pairwise interactions, agents update both their decision weights and trust estimates using an online Bayesian rule derived from teacher-student learning over a noisy channel. In-context-learning experiments with large language models qualitatively reproduce the predicted opinion and trust updates. We then assign each agent one of two arbitrary group labels, A or B, and model group bias by allowing a fraction $f_b$ of agents to shift its perceived agreement with each message by a strength $b$, a bias steering prejudice, depending on whether the sender and receiver share a label. <details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

In a hierarchical multi-agent system, a Leader assigns tasks and subagents carry them out. Jointly training the Leader and the subagents is difficult because a final answer gives only indirect feedback to the subagents. An LLM judge can score the performance of the subagents, but these scores depend on the judge's own assessment of the quality of subagents' responses. We present MultiAgent-OPD, which combines on-policy distillation with reinforcement learning to jointly train a shared-parameter Leader-Searcher-Answerer team end to end. We first train a Teacher in a Searcher-Answerer workflow using answer correctness. During team training, the frozen Teacher scores student-generated search and summary tokens to provide local process supervision for the Searchers, while the Leader learns from team success and the Answerer from candidate correctness. Across seven datasets, the trained system reaches 38.68% macro avg@4, exceeding Prompted Critic by 4.06 percentage points and C-MAS + RL, the trained cascaded multiagent system, by 2.61 percentage points. The team also solves more questions within four attempts.

<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Indivisible resources allocated in advance may need to be reallocated when capacity reductions make existing assignments infeasible, yet monetary compensation is often impractical or undesirable. We develop a sequential resource reallocation scheme using reusable artificial credits, where agents hold pre-existing resource rights and have heterogeneous marginal valuations over time blocks. Capacity reductions produce fractional endowments, which our closed-credit scheme resolves into indivisible final allocations. We model agents' intertemporal preferences through a predicted-value-to-go function and characterize conditions ensuring that their optimal demand is non-increasing in price. We propose an ascending-auction mechanism that partitions agents into subgroups and uses the minimum subgroup market-clearing price as a common transaction price. We show that the mechanism preserves non-myopic individual rationality and weak nontrader neutrality. Moreover, we establish Pareto efficiency under optimal-demand clearing in the unified market and bound the expected gain from misreporting by the probability that an agent changes the common price. Simulations of airport slot reallocation demonstrate reductions in total modeled delay and cancellation costs relative to a ground-delay baseline.

#### <span data-idx="6"></span>Tree-Aware Posterior Distillation for Long-Horizon Agentic Reinforcement Learning

> **面向长程智能体强化学习的树感知后验蒸馏**

- **arXiv**：[2610.23181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23181) ｜ **PDF**：[下载](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23181) ｜ **HTML**：[全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23181v1)
- 机构（T0）：Tsinghua University；Tencent ｜ 主要作者：Heming Zou（共 10 人）
- **arXiv 分类**：Machine Learning (cs.LG) ; Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI); Computation and Language (cs.CL)
- **摘要（中文·Agent翻译）**：可验证奖励强化学习已成为大语言模型一种可扩展的后训练范式。然而，在长程交互上训练智能体面临两项挑战：仅使用终端奖励会使回合级信用分配稀疏且高方差；而在固定 rollout 批次上进行顺序小批量更新，又会因当前策略偏离行为策略而引入策略陈旧问题。现有方法通过学习式评论家、过程奖励模型或同策略蒸馏来获得更稠密的信用信号，但它们分别依赖价值网络、步级标注或更强的外部教师模型，从而增加了训练复杂度。另一方面，重要性采样与裁剪虽然能够缓解策略漂移，却并未指明一个有限的目标，使顺序更新不至于过冲。本文提出 TreeOPSD，一个面向长程智能体强化学习的无评论家、树感知后验自蒸馏框架。TreeOPSD 将每个交互回合视为树节点，从共享前缀处分支出若干备选后续，并把终端奖励递归回传为隐式的回合级信用。随后，它将每个信用映射为由相对于冻结行为策略的似然比参数化的闭式有界教师分布。最小化该教师分布与学生策略之间的散度，即可在行为策略处沿切线方向恢复策略梯度方向，同时在顺序小批量间将更新约束在有限目标之内。实证结果表明，TreeOPSD 在多个竞争性基准与多种模型规模上提升了智能体能力，尤其是在长程任务上：例如在 Deep Research 设定下，它使 Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking-2507 的平均准确率相较 GRPO 提升 4.97 个百分点。

<details>
<summary>摘要（英文原文）</summary>

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards has become a scalable post-training paradigm for large language models. However, training agents over long-horizon interactions presents two challenges: using only terminal rewards makes turn-level credit assignment sparse and high-variance, while sequential mini-batch updates on a fixed rollout batch introduce policy staleness as the current policy drifts from the behavior policy. Existing approaches obtain denser credit via learned critics, process reward models, or on-policy distillation, but they rely on a value network, step-level annotations, or a stronger external teacher that increase training complexity. Meanwhile, importance sampling and clipping mitigate policy drift, but they do not specify a finite target that stops sequential updates from overshooting. In this work, we introduce TreeOPSD, a critic-free, tree-aware posterior self-distillation framework for long-horizon agentic reinforcement learning.
